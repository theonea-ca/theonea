About The ONEA关于 The ONEA

One room, one esthetician, one client at a time.一间护理室，一位美容师，一次只接待一位客人。

Your esthetician你的美容师

The ONEA is led by a professionally trained esthetician with a decade of hands-on skincare experience. Every appointment is with the same person, so you get consistency, attention to detail, and someone who understands your skin over time.

The ONEA 由一位接受过专业训练、拥有十年护肤实践经验的美容师主理。每一次预约都由同一个人完成，让你得到稳定的品质、细致的观察，和对你皮肤长期的了解。

Professional products, including SkinCeuticals, POLA and Dermabell.使用 SkinCeuticals、POLA、Dermabell 等专业产品。

A different way to care for your skin另一种照顾皮肤的方式

Skincare shouldn’t become a burden. You don’t need to chase every new ingredient or try every trending treatment, or worry because your skin is sometimes imperfect.

护肤，不必成为生活的负担。不需要追逐每一种新成分，不需要尝试每一项热门护理，也不需要因为皮肤偶尔不完美而感到焦虑。

Good skincare starts with understanding, not with adding more.

好的皮肤护理始于理解，而不是叠加。

Inspired by global skincare traditions. Thoughtfully refined for your skin. We start with a professional assessment, then choose targeted products, careful hands-on technique and a comfortable experience. Results and enjoyment don’t have to be a trade-off.不拘泥于单一护肤流派，汲取不同体系中值得的手法与理念。从专业的皮肤评估开始，再到针对性的产品、细致的手法与舒适的感官体验。效果与享受，不必二选一。
